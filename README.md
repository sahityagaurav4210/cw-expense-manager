## 📌 Overview

Managing day-to-day finances shouldn't require complex spreadsheets, confusing formulas, or hours of manual paperwork. **CW Expense Manager** simplifies how you manage money by bringing income allocation, daily expense logging, loan/debt recovery tracking, and formal audit statements into one cohesive, user-friendly space.

Whether you want to ensure you stay within your monthly budget, track who owes you money, or download a formal financial statement for audit and tax purposes, CW Expense Manager does the heavy lifting for you.

> **Smart, Simple & Stress-Free Financial Management**  
> A comprehensive platform designed to give individuals, freelancers, and small business owners complete clarity, control, and peace of mind over their everyday finances.

## 🎯 What Problems Does It Solve?

- **"Where did all my money go?"** — Instant visibility into every rupee spent, grouped by clear categories and spending patterns.
- **Overspending & Budget Leakage** — Visual progress meters that alert you before your monthly budget is exhausted.
- **Forgotten Loans & Borrows** — A dedicated system that reminds you of pending receivables, overdue collections, and debts you need to repay.
- **Scattered Receipts & Unorganized Records** — Keep verified digital receipts attached directly to your expenses for future verification.
- **Tax & Audit Headaches** — One-click generation of beautifully formatted, executive-ready monthly financial audit statements.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📊 1. Intelligent Dashboard & Visual Insights

- **Live Financial Snapshot**: View your monthly allocated income, total expenses incurred, and unspent money left in real time.
- **Budget Utilization Meter**: Watch your spending pace with color-coded warning indicators (Green = Economical, Orange = Normal, Red = High Burn).
- **Category-Wise Breakdown**: See the percentage and exact amount spent across categories like Food, Utilities, Travel, Shopping, and more.
- **Multiple Views**: Switch between a clean **Cards View** for numbers and an interactive **Graph View** for visual trends.

### 💰 2. Expense & Inflow Management

- **Fixed vs. Variable Tagging**: Differentiate non-negotiable commitments (rent, EMIs, insurance) from discretionary daily expenses (dining, entertainment).
- **Digital Receipt Attachments**: Attach bills and receipts to transactions so you always have proof of purchase.
- **Additional Monthly Inflows**: Record bonuses, freelance earnings, interest, or side income received throughout the month.
- **Bulk Import**: Effortlessly log multiple expenses at once without entering them one by one.

### 🤝 3. Debts & Receivables Tracker

- **Money Lent (To Recover)**: Track amounts given to friends, colleagues, or vendors. View current balance, settled portions, and overdue reminders.
- **Money Borrowed (To Repay)**: Keep an accurate ledger of what you owe, ensuring timely repayments and healthy relationships.
- **Settlement Records**: Record partial or full payments made or received via Cash, UPI, or Bank Transfer.
- **Health Indicators**: Real-time recovery and repayment ratio bars showing your overall loan settlement progress.

### 📄 4. Formal Financial Audit Statements & PDF Export

- **Corporate-Grade Statement**: Automatically compiles your monthly income, inflows, categorized spending, verified bills, and debt ledgers into a formal report.
- **Executive Summary & Observations**: Includes key financial indicators such as budget compliance, savings rate, daily average burn, and credit exposure.
- **Instant Preview & Download**: Preview your statement in real time for any chosen month and download the official document directly to your device.

### 👥 5. Integrated Contact Book

- **Financial Contacts**: Organize contacts into Friends, Family, Colleagues, Clients, and Vendors.
- **Seamless Transactions**: Quickly select contacts when logging loans, repayments, or shared expenses without retyping details.

### 🔒 6. Enterprise-Grade Security & Account Safety

- **Multi-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Safeguard your financial information using Email Verification Codes or Authenticator Apps (like Google Authenticator).
- **Verified Password Reset**: Secure account recovery process with one-time verification codes.
- **Privacy First**: Your financial data belongs exclusively to you and remains strictly confidential.

## 🧭 How It Works (Simple 4-Step Flow)

```
  1. Set Your Income Budget (Define monthly funds)
  2. Record Daily Expenses (Tag categories & bills)
  3. Track Debts & Loans (Monitor payables/lent)
  4. Review & Export (Download PDF report)
```

1. **Plan**: Allocate your monthly income or budget at the start of the month.
2. **Track**: Add expenses as they happen. Categorize them and attach receipts if needed.
3. **Monitor**: Check the dashboard to know exactly how much money remains and how loans are progressing.
4. **Reflect**: Download your end-of-month statement to review spending habits and plan better for next month.
